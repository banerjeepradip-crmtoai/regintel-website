Privacy notice

How RegIntel handles the personal data you share through this website.

Who is responsible

This website is operated by InsightBridge Labs, which is responsible for the personal data described here. For any privacy question or request, contact us through the enquiry form.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the request-a-demo form, we receive your name, work email address, company, primary interest and your message. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to continue any conversation you start with us. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries and, where relevant, taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement (GDPR Article 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b)).

Who else handles it

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through our own backend (running on Cloudflare) and Resend, an email-delivery service. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation. Links to LinkedIn and to our video on HeyGen take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting business relationship, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Contact us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
