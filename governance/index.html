Regulatory context

Designed for an evolving regulatory environment.

The roadmap references the EU AI Act, GDPR and DORA as relevant frameworks. Applicability varies by AI use case, sector, role and jurisdiction.

RegIntel aims to support evidence organization and accountable workflows. It does not itself certify legal compliance or replace legal assessment.
